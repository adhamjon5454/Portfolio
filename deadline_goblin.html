THE LORE

You are a sleep-deprived student. Collect 5 task fragments, avoid two chaotic groupmates, then reach UPLOAD. Your groupmate has been “typing…” since Tuesday.

OBJECTS ON THE BOARD

  • ✦ Task fragment — collect it
  • 👀 Groupmate — moves suspiciously
  • ☕ Coffee — restores 8 brain cells
  • ▧ Procrastination pile — blocked
  • UP Upload Portal — escape here

YOUR SPECIAL MOVE

Blame the Wi-Fi: spend one excuse token to freeze every groupmate for three turns. It does not actually fix your Wi-Fi. It just buys you a moment.

HOW TO WIN

1. Collect all 5 green ✦ fragments.
2. Reach the cyan UPLOAD tile.
Avoid: pink groupmates. They move every four turns.
Coffee: restores 8 brain cells.
Lose: brain cells reach 0 or a groupmate catches you.

QUICK TIP

Use WASD / arrow keys. Press Space to freeze groupmates for 3 of their turns. You start with 40 brain cells, so you have enough moves to finish the assignment.
